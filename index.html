About the company

Independent by design.

Cyra Press LLC is an independent Texas limited liability company creating and publishing software, educational resources, and digital reference works.

Our work is grounded in professional experience across healthcare, communication design, and technology. We focus on small, useful products that help people learn, organize information, and make complex subjects more approachable.

Cyra Press is actively developing new applications and publications for Apple platforms and the web.
